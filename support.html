Common questions

Nobody hears me when I talk

Check that the radio is On (the screen says LIVE), that you allowed microphone access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone, and that the other person is on exactly the same frequency.

How do I start or cancel my free trial?

Choose a plan on the Pro screen and tap Start Your 7-Day Free Trial. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Dolair WalkieTalkieLive Pro.

I already subscribed, but the app shows the paywall

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall (on iPhone or Apple Watch) while signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The Apple Watch app won't connect

Open WalkieTalkie Live on your iPhone first and keep your iPhone nearby. The watch sends voice clips through your iPhone.

How do I block or report someone?

Tap their name or avatar, then choose Block or Report. Blocked people can't reach you. We review reports and remove people who break the rules.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile › Settings › Delete my account. This deletes your account and your data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
