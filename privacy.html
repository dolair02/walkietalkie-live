Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy explains what the WalkieTalkie Live app for iPhone and Apple Watch (published on the App Store as "Dolair WalkieTalkieLive", "the app") collects, why, and how you can delete it. The app is operated by Dolair ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You don't need a phone number, email address, or real name to use the app.
  • Voice clips you send are shared with the people on the same frequency or channel and are set to be deleted from our servers after 24 hours.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

The microphone is used only while you hold the talk button or record a voice message. We don't record in the background.

Our hosting provider, Google Firebase, may process technical data such as your IP address and device information to run and secure the service. See Firebase's privacy information.

Purchases

Subscriptions and coin packs are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app sends your voice clips and radio settings to your paired iPhone, which sends them on in the same way described above.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Sharing

Your nickname and voice clips are visible to the other people on the same frequency or channel. Remember that anyone listening could record what they hear. Apart from that, we share information only with Google Firebase (our hosting provider), or when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

Keeping and deleting your data

Voice clips are set to expire after 24 hours. Channel activity is removed when you leave a channel. To delete everything, use Profile › Settings › Delete my account in the app. This deletes your anonymous account from our servers and erases the data stored on your device. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The app is not for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child is using the app, contact us and we'll delete their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, or to object to how it's used. Email us to make a request.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and our database rules only let your own account write as you. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't say anything in a voice clip you wouldn't want others to hear.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
